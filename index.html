Crafting Unique Spaces
Since 2011

Home Decor was established in 2011 with the vision of introducing innovative architectural materials to the market. Since its inception, the company has continuously sourced and curated extraordinary materials from around the world, ensuring customers have access to unique products that combine quality, durability, and timeless design.

Today, Home Decor has earned a reputation for supplying uncommon architectural materials that enable architects, interior designers, builders, and homeowners to create distinctive and memorable spaces.
